# OneCart Commerce video promo kit

This kit produces two matching 75–90 second videos: one in English and one in Bangla. Use actual OneCart screen recordings and screenshots. Do not use generated visuals to represent a feature the product does not have.

## Deliverables

- Landscape master: 1920 × 1080, 16:9, MP4, 30 fps — YouTube, Facebook, website.
- Vertical cut: 1080 × 1920, 9:16, MP4, 30 fps — Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts.
- English version: English narration and subtitles.
- Bangla version: Bangla narration and subtitles; retain actual software labels as shown in the application.

## Before recording

1. Start the API and storefront locally, or use a deployed demo environment.
2. Use safe demonstration data only. Do not show administrator passwords, authenticator QR codes, real customer names, phone numbers, addresses, reset links, database credentials, or SMTP passwords.
3. Prepare one published product with two or more variants, images, an active flash sale, at least one attribute group, and a published review.
4. Set the browser zoom to 100%. Record at 1920 × 1080 where possible.
5. Record each scene without sound. The narration and music will be added later.

## CapCut workflow

1. Create a new 16:9 project.
2. Import the ten clips in the listed order and trim them to the suggested duration.
3. Add the appropriate narration script using **Text to speech**. Select one clear English voice for the English cut and one natural Bangla voice for the Bangla cut. Listen to the entire result; correct any brand-name or technical-word pronunciation manually.
4. Turn on **Auto captions** in the same language as the narration. Correct all captions; do not rely on auto-captions for Bangla spelling.
5. Add subtle, licensed background music at roughly 10–15% volume under the narration. Use only music licensed for your intended distribution.
6. Use short fade or dissolve transitions (about 0.3–0.5 seconds). Avoid excessive effects.
7. Add the English or Bangla on-screen captions above. Use high contrast and keep all text within safe margins.
8. Export the landscape master as MP4, 1080p, 30 fps. Duplicate the project, switch to 9:16, reframe clips, and export the vertical version.

## CapCut AI project prompt

Paste this prompt when starting an AI-assisted draft, then replace its suggested footage with your actual recordings:

```text
Create a polished 85-second software product promo. Use a clean, modern, trustworthy commercial style with rose, white, and dark accent colors. The product is OneCart Commerce, a single-product commerce platform. Use these scenes in order: storefront hero; selecting variants and attributes; cart and cash-on-delivery checkout; admin dashboard; product, price and inventory management; storefront branding and translations; order management; administrator security; closing logo and WhatsApp contact. Keep pacing professional, use subtle transitions, reserve readable space for captions, and do not invent product features. Use the supplied screen recordings as the primary visuals.
```

## Final review before publishing

- Confirm all claims match the live product.
- Confirm no sensitive information appears in the footage or captions.
- Check the Bangla text with a Bangla-speaking reviewer and the English text with an English-speaking reviewer.
- Watch once on a mobile phone with sound off; captions must remain readable.
- Watch once with sound on; narration must be clear and music must not overpower it.
- Export separate files with clear names, for example `onecart-promo-en-1080p.mp4` and `onecart-promo-bn-1080p.mp4`.
